# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective and existing legal clients navigating legal matters in Portugal — primarily Portuguese, Brazilian, and Chinese individuals and companies. They often arrive stressed, uncertain, or overwhelmed by legal complexity, and many are making high-stakes decisions (citizenship, immigration, property, business formation). A large share are foreigners for whom Portuguese law and language are unfamiliar, so being understood in their own language matters as much as the legal outcome. They need to trust quickly: the site must convey competence immediately while feeling approachable enough that they actually reach out.

## Product Purpose

The marketing website for Bárbara Barbizani's law practice. It exists to turn stressed, uncertain visitors into booked consultations by establishing her expertise and warmth before the first conversation. Success is a visitor who feels understood and confident enough to request a consultation — and, secondarily, one who stays to explore her story and practice areas until they are ready.

## Positioning

A lawyer who speaks your language — literally (Portuguese, English, Chinese) and figuratively — guiding foreigners and locals through Portuguese law with genuine expertise. Every page reinforces that you will be understood *and* well represented.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Book / request a consultation.
- Secondary CTA: Read her story on the About page — for visitors not yet ready to book, deepening trust before the ask.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "A lawyer who speaks my language and knows Portuguese law inside out."
- Belief ladder (in order): (1) "She understands my situation and speaks my language." (2) "She is genuinely expert and will get results." — the make-or-break belief for this audience. (3) "I can reach out without feeling intimidated."
- Proof on hand: client testimonials and experience stats (years, cases, clients). Published articles serve as supporting credibility.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, warm, refined. Elegant minimalism — prestigious enough to feel expert, warm enough to feel human. The voice is calm and reassuring, never cold, flashy, or generic. It should evoke four feelings at once: confidence ("I'm in safe, capable hands"), calm ("she will handle the stress for me"), prestige ("this is a high-caliber professional"), and approachability ("I can reach out without intimidation").

## Anti-references

No glassmorphism, gradient text, neon accents, or dark-mode-by-default. No generic AI aesthetics (purple-to-blue gradients, Inter font, card-heavy layouts). No playful animations, bouncy easing, or whimsical copy — this is a law firm. No visual impact at the expense of readability. Colors stay within the navy / gold / cream palette; new colors are introduced only after discussion.

## Design Principles

Authority through restraint — every element earns its place; the navy-and-gold palette does the heavy lifting rather than added decoration. Understood in your language — bilingual (and multilingual) parity is a first-class concern, never an afterthought; layouts and copy work equally in PT and EN. Calm the stress — the experience should lower a worried visitor's anxiety, guiding them in sequence rather than overwhelming them. Approachable prestige — signal high caliber while staying human and reachable; warmth lives in the details (gold accents, hover states, focus rings). Earn trust before the ask — establish competence and warmth first, so the consultation request feels like a natural next step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Semantic HTML, proper focus management, and color contrast that holds for all text across the navy/gold/cream palette. Every interactive element is keyboard-navigable. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Bilingual by design: content must accommodate both Portuguese and English without breaking layouts.
