Divorce is one of the most sensitive decisions in a family's life. Understanding your options helps reduce the emotional strain and protect what matters most — especially the children. Here are the essentials for **Barreiro** families.

## Two paths to divorce

**By mutual consent** — when both parties agree. This is the fastest route and can be handled at the Civil Registry Office, provided there is agreement on the essential points.

**Contested** — when there is no agreement. It goes through the courts and, as a rule, requires representation by a lawyer.

## Custody and parental responsibilities

Where there are minor children, you must define:

- Who they usually live with;
- The arrangements for time with the other parent;
- How important decisions about the child's life are shared.

The guiding criterion is always the **best interests of the child**.

## Child support

Child support is intended to cover the child's expenses and is set according to **two factors**: the needs of the person receiving it and the means of the person paying it. It can be revised if circumstances change — for example, a significant change in income.

> A well-drafted agreement prevents future conflict. It is worth investing time at this stage.
