Losing your job is always a difficult moment. But the first days after a **dismissal** are decisive: they are when your rights are protected. If you work in Portugal, here is what you should do.

## 1. Ask for everything in writing

Demand a written dismissal notice stating the grounds. Without valid, communicated grounds, a dismissal may be considered **unlawful**.

## 2. Check your final accounts

At the end of the contract you are generally entitled to:

- Pay for the days worked;
- Accrued and untaken holidays and the corresponding allowance;
- Pro-rata holidays, holiday allowance, and Christmas allowance for the year of termination;
- Severance, where applicable to the type of dismissal.

## 3. Watch the deadlines

A judicial challenge to a dismissal is subject to **short deadlines**. Letting the deadline pass may mean losing your right to react, even if you are in the right.

> If you believe the dismissal was unfair, seek legal advice immediately. Here, time works against you.

## 4. Keep the evidence

Your contract, payslips, messages, and the dismissal letter are essential to assess your case.
